01 — About

I bridge the gap between autonomous deep learning systems and high-performance backend pipelines. That means training models, then building what lets them survive production: memory, tracing, evaluation and infrastructure that stays up.

I am a B.Tech student at the Indian Institute of Technology Patna, class of 2028. My degree is in Chemical Science and Technology. My time goes to machine learning, distributed systems and robots.

I coordinate the NJACK ML Club, manage its Substack magazine The Learning Curve, and have twice reached the DD Robocon national finals with IIT Patna’s robotics team.
